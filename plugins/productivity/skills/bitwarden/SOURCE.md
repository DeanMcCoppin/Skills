## Notes

- Verdict: the best open-source password manager. The free or Premium plan is plenty for Dean. The real dev win is Secrets Manager or the CLI to keep keys out of .env, chat and git.
- Good at:
  - end-to-end encryption
  - free plan with unlimited passwords and devices
  - cheap Premium
  - Send for secure handovers
  - Organizations for clients
  - CLI and Secrets Manager
- Weak at / risks:
  - the official self-host is heavy (Vaultwarden is unofficial)
  - some UX lags 1Password
  - Secrets Manager pricing not verified here
  - the promo's SOC 2/HIPAA claims apply to Bitwarden's cloud, not to self-hosting
- Alternatives: 1Password (best UX, paid); Proton Pass; KeePassXC (local files); Vaultwarden (self-host)
