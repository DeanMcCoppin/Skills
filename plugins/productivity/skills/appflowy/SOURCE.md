## Notes

- Verdict: a solid local-first, private Notion alternative for Dean's own notes and project wikis. Notion is still better for heavy collaboration and integrations.
- Good at:
  - local-first, offline
  - docs, databases, kanban, calendar
  - local AI with Ollama
  - free desktop app
  - self-hostable cloud
- Weak at / risks:
  - free cloud plan is tiny (100 MB)
  - fewer integrations and templates than Notion
  - self-hosted seat terms need checking
  - the promo's repo is a third-party example, not the official one
- Alternatives: Notion (polish, ecosystem); Obsidian (Markdown files, plugins); AFFiNE (open source, whiteboard + docs); Anytype (local-first)
