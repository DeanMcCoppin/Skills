## Notes

- Verdict: the go-to for premium marketing and content sites for a specific business. Not meant for app UIs, dashboards or playful brands.
- Good at: typography-led, photo-led layouts with restrained color and few, deliberate moves; 9 mood presets plus page sets per industry; a build order that keeps the theme reskinnable.
- Weak at / risks: it only looks as good as the photography, so placeholders look flat; editorial restraint can feel cold for kids, food delivery or gaming brands; the style is widely imitated.
- Alternatives: buying Lexington's themes (Astro, commercial license, one-time payment, listed at $99 personal / $299 team at review) if you'd rather start from finished code; `dean-design-taste` alone for non-theme UI work.
- Review: style observed from the templates page and the Carrington, Astromax and Matterhaus demos on 2026-10-27 (fonts: Switzer, General Sans, Inter + Newsreader; OKLCH neutrals; square corners; 150–700ms ease-out motion).
