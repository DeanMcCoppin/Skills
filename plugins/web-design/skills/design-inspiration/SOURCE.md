## Notes

- Verdict: free galleries for marketing sites and brand looks; use `refero` for product screens and flows. They complement each other rather than overlap.
- landings.dev: free gallery of real landing pages. Browse by stack (`/stack/...`) and color (`/color/...`); each post links to the live site (`?ref=landings.dev`). Has a "Clone in Claude Code" button. Don't use it for client work, because cloning goes against the no-cloning rule. No category filter for sections (hero, pricing) seen on 2026-10-30.
- rebrand.gallery: curated rebrand case studies (reveal video, logo, identity) plus "Shots" of applied work. Filters: industry, style, typography, typeface, mood. Free: 778 brands, 200 shots/day, limited search, 1 collection, AI search, **Rebrand MCP (beta)**. Pro: all 14,723 shots, unlimited search and collections, from ~€5/mo (exact price was garbled on the pricing page), 30% off yearly. MCP setup page not found at /mcp; look it up on the site before connecting.
- ilovecreatives: mainly a creative trade school (courses), job board and newsletter. The inspiration value is its curated favourite-websites lists and Squarespace template demos. Weak for SaaS or conversion patterns.
- ui8.net: marketplace for UI kits, templates, illustrations and 3D. Paid per item or All-Access subscription (price not verified: Cloudflare blocked every automated fetch on 2026-10-30). A third-party summary says the standard license allows client work but not resale; confirm against UI8's own license per product.
- Alternatives: `refero` (product UI, MCP, Pro $120/yr), Mobbin, Dribbble/Behance (less realistic), Awwwards/Godly (showcase sites).
- Review: sites checked 2026-10-30 (landings.dev home and a post, rebrand.gallery home and pricing, ilovecreatives home); UI8 not reachable by bots.
